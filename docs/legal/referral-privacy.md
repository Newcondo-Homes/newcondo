NewCondo Referral Program Privacy Policy
Effective Date: January 1, 2026
Last Updated: December 27, 2025
1. Introduction
This Referral Privacy Policy ("Policy") explains how NewCondo Limited ("NewCondo", "we", "us") collects, uses, and protects personal data in connection with our Referral Program. This Policy supplements our main Privacy Policy.
2. Data Controller
NewCondo Limited
Lagos, Nigeria
Email: privacy@newcondo.ng
Data Protection Officer: dpo@newcondo.ng
3. Information We Collect
3.1 Referral Tracking Data
When you participate in the Referral Program, we collect:
Click Data:

Timestamp of click
IP address
User agent (browser and device information)
Referrer URL (where the click came from)
Session identifier
Geographic location (country and city)

Referral Information:

Your unique referral code
Referred user's email address (after signup)
Referred user's phone number (after signup)
Relationship between referrer and referred
Referral status and qualification date

Conversion Events:

Signup timestamp
First transaction details
Transaction amounts (for commission calculation)
Payment methods used

3.2 Attribution Data

Cookies and similar technologies
Session tracking across devices
User journey through the platform
Conversion path analysis

3.3 Reward Information

Rewards earned and their amounts
Redemption history
Virtual account balances
Payout requests and bank details

4. How We Use Your Data
4.1 Primary Uses
We use referral data for:
Attribution Tracking:

Link referrals to specific users
Track conversion events
Calculate rewards accurately

Fraud Prevention:

Detect duplicate accounts
Identify suspicious patterns
Prevent referral abuse
Protect program integrity

Performance Analytics:

Measure program effectiveness
Generate performance reports
Optimize reward structures
Improve user experience

Commission Calculation:

Calculate referral rewards
Process agent commissions
Manage payout schedules
Track virtual account balances

4.2 Secondary Uses
With your consent, we may use data for:

Marketing communications about referral opportunities
Personalized referral suggestions
Program improvement surveys

5. Legal Basis for Processing
We process referral data based on:
Contract Performance:

Processing necessary to fulfill the Referral Program agreement
Calculate and deliver rewards
Manage your virtual account

Legitimate Interests:

Fraud prevention and security
Program analytics and optimization
Business operations and reporting

Consent:

Marketing communications
Optional tracking features

Legal Obligations:

Tax reporting requirements

Regulatory reporting

6. Data Sharing & Disclosure
6.1 What We Don't Share
We do NOT share:

Your personal contact information with referred users
Your full name or identity with referred users
Banking details with third parties (except payment processors)

6.2 What We May Share
With Referred Users:

Your first name only (e.g., "John invited you")
Your referral relationship (if they accept)
No contact information or full identity

With Service Providers:

Payment processors (for payouts)
Analytics platforms (anonymized data)
Fraud detection services
Email/SMS providers (for notifications)

With Authorities:

When required by Nigerian law
In response to valid legal requests
To protect our rights or safety

In Business Transfers:

If NewCondo is acquired or merged
Data transferred to successor entity

7. Data Retention
7.1 Retention Periods
Active Referrals:

Retained while referral is pending or active
Minimum: Until qualification or expiration

Conversion Data:

Retained permanently for accounting
Required for tax and audit purposes

Click Data:

Retained for 90 days
Deleted if no conversion occurs

Reward Records:

Retained for 7 years after payout
Required for financial compliance

7.2 Deletion Requests
You may request deletion of:

Click tracking data (after 90 days)
Marketing preferences (immediate)

Cannot delete:

Qualified referral records (legal requirement)
Financial transaction history
Fraud detection data

8. Your Rights
Under the Nigerian Data Protection Regulation (NDPR), you have the right to:
8.1 Access

Request a copy of your referral data
View your referral history and rewards
Access your virtual account statements

8.2 Correction

Update incorrect personal information
Correct referral relationship errors
Update payment details

8.3 Deletion

Request deletion of non-essential data
Remove yourself from the Program
Delete account (subject to legal requirements)

8.4 Objection

Object to processing for marketing
Opt-out of referral tracking
Withdraw consent (where applicable)

8.5 Data Portability

Receive your data in machine-readable format
Transfer data to another service

8.6 Restriction

Request restriction of processing
Temporarily suspend your participation

To Exercise Your Rights:
Email: privacy@newcondo.ng
Subject: "Referral Data Rights Request"
