# NewCondo Referral Program Terms & Conditions

**Effective Date:** January 1, 2026  
**Last Updated:** December 27, 2025

## 1. Program Overview

The NewCondo Referral Program ("Program") allows eligible users to earn rewards by referring new users to the NewCondo platform. By participating in the Program, you agree to these Terms and Conditions.

## 2. Eligibility

### 2.1 Who Can Refer
- Registered NewCondo users with active accounts
- Property owners with paid subscriptions
- Verified agents on the platform
- Premium renters (subscription required)
- Account must be in good standing

### 2.2 Who Cannot Participate
- Suspended or banned accounts
- Users with pending verification issues
- Accounts flagged for fraudulent activity
- NewCondo employees and contractors (unless explicitly approved)

## 3. Referral Rules

### 3.1 Valid Referrals
- Referred user must be new to NewCondo
- Referred user must not have previously registered
- Referred user must complete signup using your unique referral code
- One referral code per new user
- No self-referrals permitted

### 3.2 Prohibited Activities
- Creating fake or duplicate accounts
- Using automated tools or bots
- Purchasing or selling referral codes
- Spamming referral links
- Circular referrals (A refers B, B refers A)
- Any form of gaming the system

### 3.3 Maximum Referrals
- No limit on total number of referrals
- Rate limits apply: 10 referrals per day, 3 per hour
- Excessive referral activity subject to fraud review

## 4. Reward Structure

### 4.1 Referral Types and Rewards

**Owner → Owner**
- Referrer receives: ₦10,000 service credit
- Referred receives: ₦10,000 service credit

**Owner → Agent**
- Referrer receives: ₦5,000 service credit
- Referred receives: ₦5,000 service credit

**Owner → Renter**
- Referrer receives: ₦2,000 service credit
- Referred receives: ₦2,000 service credit

**Agent → Owner**
- Referrer receives: ₦5,000 commission credit
- Referred receives: ₦5,000 service credit

**Agent → Agent**
- Referrer receives: ₦3,000 commission credit
- Referred receives: ₦3,000 commission credit

**Agent → Renter**
- Referrer receives: ₦5,000 commission credit
- Referred receives: ₦5,000 rent credit

**Renter → Renter**
- Referrer receives: ₦2,000 rent credit
- Referred receives: ₦2,000 rent credit

### 4.2 Milestone Bonuses
Additional rewards for reaching referral milestones:
- 5 qualified referrals: ₦5,000 bonus
- 10 qualified referrals: ₦10,000 bonus
- 25 qualified referrals: ₦25,000 bonus
- 50 qualified referrals: ₦50,000 bonus
- 100 qualified referrals: ₦100,000 bonus

## 5. Qualification Criteria

### 5.1 When Referrals Qualify
A referral is qualified when the referred user:
1. Completes account registration
2. Verifies their email and phone number
3. Completes their first transaction on the platform:
   - Property owners: Subscribe to a paid plan
   - Agents: List their first property or complete marking job
   - Renters: Make their first rent payment

### 5.2 Qualification Timeframe
- Referred user has 90 days from signup to qualify
- Referrals not qualified within 90 days expire automatically
- Rewards issued only after qualification

## 6. Reward Redemption

### 6.1 How to Use Rewards
Service credits can be applied to:
- Property rental payments
- Subscription fees
- Maintenance services
- Property marking services
- Premium feature upgrades

### 6.2 Reward Delivery
- Rewards credited to virtual account after qualification
- Available for immediate use or withdrawal
- Minimum withdrawal amount: ₦5,000
- Withdrawal to bank account within 1-3 business days

### 6.3 Reward Expiry
- Rewards expire 365 days after issuance
- Reminder notifications sent at 30, 7, 3, and 1 day before expiry
- Expired rewards are forfeited and cannot be recovered
- Extensions may be granted at NewCondo's sole discretion

## 7. Fraud Prevention

### 7.1 Fraud Detection
NewCondo employs automated fraud detection systems that monitor:
- IP addresses and device fingerprints
- Signup patterns and timing
- Account relationships
- Transaction behaviors

### 7.2 Consequences of Fraud
Violation of Program rules may result in:
- Immediate suspension from the Program
- Forfeiture of pending rewards
- Account suspension or termination
- Legal action for severe violations

### 7.3 Appeals
Users may appeal fraud decisions by contacting support@newcondo.ng with relevant evidence within 14 days.

## 8. Privacy & Data Usage

### 8.1 Data Collection
NewCondo collects referral data including:
- Click timestamps and IP addresses
- Device information and user agents
- Referral relationships
- Conversion events

### 8.2 Data Usage
Referral data is used for:
- Attribution tracking
- Fraud prevention
- Performance analytics
- Commission calculation

### 8.3 Data Sharing
- Referral codes and links may contain your user ID
- No personal information shared with referred users
- Aggregate data may be used for reporting
- Compliance with Nigerian Data Protection Regulation (NDPR)

## 9. Program Changes

### 9.1 Modifications
NewCondo reserves the right to:
- Modify reward amounts with 30 days notice
- Change qualification criteria
- Update Program rules
- Discontinue the Program

### 9.2 Notification
Changes will be communicated via:
- Email notification to all participants
- In-app announcements
- Website updates

### 9.3 Existing Referrals
Changes do not affect already qualified referrals or issued rewards.

- Service credits have no cash value outside NewCondo

- NewCondo may adjust credit values for pricing changes

### 10.2 Tax Obligations

### 10.3 No Guarantee

## 11. Dispute Resolution

### 11.1 Contact Support
First, contact support@newcondo.ng with:
